# Solving the Problem of Copying Without Management Support
* Encourage peer to peer engagement wherein we monitor each other's work and create a culture where honesty is valued.
* Give practical tasks that are harder to copy.
* Rotate the assignments so that we don't have identical questions.
* Use rechnology to randomize quizzes to reduce copying.
* Reward orginality and effort with recognition.
* Promote discussions where flow of ideas are voiced and matter more than written answers
* Assist each other in case we are stuck to ensure we learn from our mistakes and do not repeat it again.
